About

Where clarity comes from noise

I'm a Data Analyst and recent M.S. Computer Science graduate from California State University, Fullerton. I genuinely enjoy the moment when messy, real-world data finally starts to make sense — and I've learned to be suspicious of numbers that look too clean.

That role taught me something I still lean on: an analysis is only as trustworthy as the data behind it.

I'm currently looking for entry-level Data Analyst, Business Analyst, or Product Analyst roles — and I'm open to AI/ML-focused positions — where I can dig into real problems and help teams make decisions they can trust.
